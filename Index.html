Study Website for iPhone: One File, Free, No Subscription This is the simple version made for an iPhone. Everything is in ONE file called index.html. It has a separate page for Home, Physics, Maths, Computer, English, Urdu, Islamiyat, Tarjuma-tul-Quran, Exam Prep and 3D Diagrams. Step 1: Get your free AI key (2 minutes) 1. Open Safari on your iPhone and go to aistudio.google.com 2. Sign in with your Google account. 3. Tap Get API key, then Create API key. 4. Tap the key to copy it. Keep it safe, do not share it. Step 2: Copy the code 1. Scroll down to the big code block below. 2. Press and hold on the code, choose Select All, then Copy. 3. In the code, find the words PASTE_YOUR_KEY_HERE and replace them with your key. To do this easily, paste the whole code into the Notes app first, edit it there, then copy it again. Step 3: Put it online for free 1. In Safari go to github.com and make a free account. 2. Tap the plus icon, then New repository. Name it study. Choose Public. Tap Create. 3. Tap Add file, then Create new file. Name it index.html and paste your edited code. 4. Tap Commit changes. 5. Go to Settings, then Pages. Under Branch choose main and tap Save. 6. After about two minutes your site is live at yourusername.github.io/study 7. In Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and it works like an app. Note: a public repository shows your key to anyone who looks very closely.
